Field suggestions

  • Focus manually at infinity using live-view zoomed on a bright star, not the lens's hard stop.
  • Shoot 1 stop down from wide-open if coma/astigmatism smears corner stars — e.g. f/2.8 lens at f/3.2–f/4.
  • Use a 2s timer or remote release to kill shutter-press shake; lock up mirror on DSLRs.
  • Shoot RAW — you'll push shadows hard in post and need the dynamic range.
  • Bring a dew heater strap or hand warmers for the lens barrel on humid nights.
  • Widest aperture your lens allows (low f-number) trades some corner sharpness for shorter, trail-free exposures — worth it for star points.
  • Whichever exposure time you land on, take a test frame and zoom to 100% on a corner star to confirm no trailing before committing to a sequence.
